Privacy Policy

Linnea is a personal wardrobe app. Your data is yours; here is everything we do with it.

What we store

Your account email, the photos of clothing items you add, the details you attach to them (category, colors, tags, sizes, storage boxes), your style preferences from onboarding (age range, body shape, contrast, palettes, brands, values), whether you let your stylist use your country and those preferences, the days you open the app (to count active users), and — only if you choose to provide them — body measurements, gender and fit preferences. Everything is stored with Supabase (our database and storage provider) in access-controlled storage: only your signed-in account can read your data.

Subscriptions

If you subscribe to Linnea Plus, the purchase is handled by Apple. We use RevenueCat to check whether your subscription is active; they receive your purchase record and an anonymous identifier tied to your account. We never see or store your payment details — those stay with Apple.

AI features

When you add an item or request an outfit suggestion, the relevant photo or wardrobe details are sent to Anthropic's Claude API to generate tags or styling advice, together with today's date for a suggestion. Only if you allow it (Profile → Style in the app), a suggestion also sends your country (as you typed it, or your phone's region setting) and your style answers: body shape, contrast, colour palettes, values and brands. Your age range and gender are never sent. This data is used to produce your result and is not used to train AI models (we use Anthropic's commercial API, which excludes customer data from training by default).

What we don't do

No ads. No selling or sharing your data with data brokers. No third-party analytics or trackers: the only usage we record is the days you open the app, kept by us and never shared. No tracking you across other apps or websites.

Sign in with Apple or Google

If you use them, we receive only your sign-in identity (Apple may give us a private relay email instead of your real one — that works fine). When you delete an account that signs in with Apple, we also revoke Linnea's access to your Apple ID.

Deleting your data

Delete any item and its photo is removed from storage. To delete your entire account and all data instantly, including the record of the days you opened the app, use Profile → Delete account in the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date below updates and material changes are shown in the app.
